# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text applies, this page summarises it and says how to report a problem.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in SolverMapp to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to numerical methods, to programming or to open source.
- Respect different fields and conventions: notation, units and methods differ between disciplines.
- Give and accept constructive criticism of ideas and code, not of people.
- Credit the work of others, including the authors of the equations and papers you bring in.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone
represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at **duruceren1952@gmail.com**. All reports are reviewed
promptly and fairly, and the privacy and safety of the reporter are respected.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may correct, warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who violates this code, as
described in the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
